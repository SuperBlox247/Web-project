Getting There

Yellowstone has five entrances. Choose the one closest to the sights you want to see first.

  • North Entrance (Gardiner, Montana): closest to Mammoth Hot Springs
  • Northeast Entrance (Cooke City, Montana): the gateway to Lamar Valley
  • East Entrance (near Cody, Wyoming): passes Yellowstone Lake
  • South Entrance (from Grand Teton National Park): a scenic drive between two parks
  • West Entrance (West Yellowstone, Montana): the quickest route to Old Faithful

When to Visit

Summer is the most popular season. All park roads are open, and the weather is warm, but popular areas can be crowded. Fall brings fewer visitors and active wildlife. In winter, most park roads close to regular cars, and visitors explore by guided snowcoach or snowmobile. Check the current park conditions for exact road opening and closing dates before you travel.
